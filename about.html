About Borabu Builders

Building Excellence Through Innovation, Integrity & Quality Workmanship.

Who We Are

We at Borabu Builders are engaged in the Business of Construction. We construct all manner of structures ranging from Offices and Educational facilities, to Residential Dwellings and Industrial Facilities. We also engage in construction of Water Works as well as Roads and Roads Maintenance.

From concept to completion, we work closely with our clients to ensure every project is delivered safely, on time and within budget.

Our History

Borabu Builders Company Limited was founded in Kericho County, as Anyoka Borabu Builders, a Sole Proprietorship in 1988. In our modest span of existence we have had the previlege of working with some of the leading agro-industrial firms in the immediate and neighbouring counties. Our clientele has largely comprised firms such as Browns East Africa Plantations PLC (formerly Unilever Tea Kenya Limited), Browns Plantations Kenya Limited (formerly James Finlay (KENYA) Ltd.), Kenya Tea Development Agencies, Kenya Tea Packers and Geaorge Williamson. we have also had the enlightening opportunity to work with the Government of Kenya through its various State Departments as well as Non-governmental Organisations such as Walter Reed. Projects undertaken with the aforementioned entities have included both civil structures and buildingssuch as factory construction and extensions, construction of learning facilities including storey classroom blocks and laboratories, dormitories and staff houses among others. Civil works we have undertaken have included construction of dams, furrows, Hydro-electric powerhouses and even water treatment works; and rehabilitation of roads under the Kenya Rural Roads Authority mainteneance programme.
